Knight, Mage, and Rogue_Hooded rigs are adapted for Thorvak, Ilyra, Kaelis, and Seravin. Seravin uses the Mage rig with a different costume/accessory selection and tint. Kaelis carries the pack's ranged crossbow. Only gameplay-relevant animation clips are retained in the optimized GLBs; skeletons, mesh attributes, and embedded source textures are preserved.
